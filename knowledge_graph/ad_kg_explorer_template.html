Alzheimer's disease knowledge graph

AlzKG Explorer

Seven AD-specific datasets are filtered, mapped onto shared identifiers, and joined into one graph anchored on Alzheimer's disease (MONDO:0004975). The bench below shows its core: the most strongly supported genes, variants, subtypes and drugs.

How the graph is built

1

Pull AD evidence from each source

2

Harmonize identifiers

Each source names the same thing differently. Every node is mapped to one canonical id.

  • GenesAPOE · 348 · PA55 → NCBIGene:348
  • VariantsRS#:429358 · ClinVar · GWAS → rs429358
  • ADMESH:D000544 · PA443319 · "Probable Alzheimer's Disease" → MONDO:0004975
  • Drugsmerged by normalized name
3

Restrict to the AD neighborhood

Keep edges within two hops of an AD node: direct evidence plus the second hop that matters for repurposing (gene → drug).
